Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you book. Anything else — call (862) 350-2221.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing and electrical service?+

Yes. Burst pipes, major leaks, sewer backups, power problems and sparking panels can't wait, so a licensed technician is on call around the clock in Montclair and the surrounding Essex County towns. Call (862) 350-2221 any time, day or night.

How does your upfront flat-rate pricing work?+

We diagnose the problem first, then give you one flat price for the whole job before any work begins. The price doesn't change with the clock — no hourly meters, no surprise add-ons. If we find something unexpected, we show you and get your approval before doing anything extra.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in Montclair, NJ and serve Bloomfield, Glen Ridge, Verona, Cedar Grove, West Orange, Livingston, Maplewood, South Orange, Nutley, Clifton and Caldwell.

How long does a water heater replacement take?+

Most standard water heater replacements are finished the same day, usually in two to four hours, including hauling away the old unit. We install traditional tank and high-efficiency tankless models, and estimates on replacements are free.

Do I need a permit for an electrical panel upgrade?+

Yes — panel upgrades in New Jersey require a permit and inspection. We handle the permit paperwork, coordinate with the utility and the inspector, and make sure the finished panel passes code, so you don't have to deal with any of it.

Can you install a whole-house generator?+

Yes. We size the generator to your home, install it with an automatic transfer switch, and take care of the electrical permits and inspections. Your lights, heat, refrigerator and sump pump stay on automatically when the power goes out.

Is your work guaranteed?+

Every job is covered by our workmanship guarantee and satisfaction guarantee: if something we installed or repaired isn't right, we come back and make it right. We're licensed and insured in New Jersey, and all work is done to code.
